Jjigeo Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Jjigeo does not collect your personal information. There is no account, no server, and no advertising or analytics tool. The photos you take and what the app reads from them are stored only on your phone, and Jjigeo does not send them anywhere.

1. Information we collect

None. Jjigeo does not ask you to sign up and does not transmit any personal information to us, including your name, email address, phone number, advertising ID or location. It contains no analytics SDK, advertising SDK or crash-reporting SDK.

Jjigeo does not connect to the network. The Android app does not include the internet permission at all.

2. Where your photos and their contents are kept

Text in a photo is read on your phone. Everything needed to read it ships inside the app, and photos are not sent to a server or to an outside AI service.

For travel and scenery, food, and kids' drawing or pet photos, the date the photo was taken is read from the photo file on your phone and offered for the date field. It goes into the record only when you tap "Use this date". The location (GPS) stored in a photo file is not read.

The photos you save, the text read from them, the values you confirm or correct, and the history of actions you run are kept in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read that area, and the data is encrypted with a key held in the phone's secure storage.

Photos and text you share into Jjigeo from another app are kept the same way under "Received items" until you process or delete them. A photo you cancel without saving is not kept. Temporary files made in order to read text or hand something to another app are deleted after use, and the leftovers are cleaned up when the app starts.

Appointment slips, business cards and receipts may contain names, phone numbers and addresses. Jjigeo only keeps that content on your phone; we cannot see it. You can black out any part you don't want to keep before saving.

3. When information leaves your phone

Only when you start it yourself. Jjigeo never sends anything on its own.

Opening calendar, contacts, maps, phone or email The values you confirmed are filled into that app's editing screen. Saving or sending is done by you in that app. What is handed over is then covered by that app's policy.
Sharing to another app The confirmed details go to the app you choose. You decide whether the photo goes too; location, capture time and other metadata are removed from a shared photo.
Exporting files You can create event, contact and expense files, and a full export file. These files contain your records as they are. A full export can be protected with a password. You decide where to store it and who receives it.
More from us Tapping the card in Settings hands a web address to your browser. The tap is not recorded or transmitted.

4. Device backup

Android's automatic backup is turned off for this app so that your records are not uploaded to the cloud. To move to a new phone, use the full export and import in Settings.

5. Permissions

Jjigeo does not request camera, photo library, contacts, calendar, location, microphone or notification permissions.

Taking a photoThe phone's camera app is opened and only the one photo you take is returned.
Choosing a photoThe system photo picker is used. Jjigeo can access only the photo you pick.
Calendar and contactsJjigeo opens those apps' editing screens, so it does not read or write your calendar or contacts.

6. Sharing with third parties

We do not share anything. We collect no information, so there is nothing to hand to a third party.

Text recognition uses Google's ML Kit library, bundled in the app. It runs on your phone, and because Jjigeo has no internet permission it cannot send photos or usage information out.

7. Children's privacy

Jjigeo does not collect personal information from anyone, regardless of age.

8. Deleting your data

You can delete records one at a time in the app, or remove all photos and records at once with "Delete all data" in Settings. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the phone.

Events and contacts saved in other apps, exported files and anything you shared are outside Jjigeo, so you need to delete those yourself.

Your records are encrypted with a key on this phone, and we do not hold that key. If you lose the phone or uninstall the app, we cannot restore your records.

9. Changes to this policy

Changes will be posted on this page with a new date. If a feature that moves data off your phone is ever added, this document will be updated first and the app will tell you as well.
